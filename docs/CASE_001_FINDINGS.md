# Case 001 research note: proposed informatics vs documented delivery

**Updated:** November 6, 2026.  
**Editorial status:** preliminary, source-linked; not a definitive forensic accounting or software-infrastructure audit.

## Question

Did Southern Trust Company establish and run the biomedical data-mining platform proposed in 2012?

## Documentary stages

| Date | Narrow finding | Reference | What is NOT proven |
|---|---|---|---|
| November 15, 2012 | A 2023 court-filed expert report describes Southern Trust's business plan and cites sworn 2012 EDC testimony about medical/financial algorithms and a proposed DNA database. | Irwin report, Case 1:22-cv-10904-JSR, Document 290-7, paragraphs 74–77 | That an operational platform existed. |
| February 3, 2015 | Richard Kahn requested an invoice to Southern Trust for algorithmic DNA-database management. | EFTA01191403 | That any vendor was paid or deployed code. |
| February 4, 2015 | Novamente issued an invoice for $30,000 describing machine learning and automated reasoning for gene expression/SNP analysis, and installation and training. | EFTA01191405 | That the invoice was settled or deliverables accepted. |
| February 5, 2015 | Kahn asked whether to pay immediately or wait for servers and software availability (estimated at two to three weeks). | EFTA01191403 | A completed payment, delivery or installation. |
| June 15, 2015 | Goertzel told Epstein his team had not installed its software on a Virgin Islands server because remote access was not configured. | EFTA00655735 | That the server was the invoiced machine, or the installation later succeeded. |
| April 12, 2023 | USVI's amended complaint alleged Southern Trust failed to perform the represented informatics business. | USVI v. JPMorgan, Document 119-1, allegations ¶¶157–176 | These allegations are not automatic judicial findings. |
| August 18, 2023 | A report prepared for JPMorgan's defense challenged EDC oversight and described the 2012 testimony. | Document 290-7 (authored June 23) | A judicial determination of whether an operational DNA platform existed. |

The court documents are **litigation positions**: the complaint contains government allegations and the expert report was prepared for JPMorgan's legal counsel. Their presence together is important to avoid one-sided conclusions.

## New insight

The **invoice request text** is more specific than generic references to "AI funding." It connects a named payer, vendor, requested service and technical domain, while the **February 5 payment question** contemporaneously shows that payment and delivery status were under discussion. That makes it inappropriate to present February 2015 as proof of a deployed DNA database.

The original file EFTA01191405 includes banking information; Pattern Atlas deliberately does not reproduce those details.
